Projects

eyEar: An AI Navigation Wearable for the Blind

MIT Solve Top 10 National Finalist

Built a $50 camera clip for glasses using a XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense, a Flutter app, and Gemini. One button press sends a photo over Bluetooth; the app matches it against Cortex, a 100+ scenario database co-designed with three blind users, and speaks back an action like "empty seat, 2 o'clock, 4 steps" in under two seconds.

Continual Reinforcement Learning for Algal Carbon Capture

WSSEF 1st Place Robotics, Top 20 of 1,100

Nine bioreactors, each controlled by a Raspberry Pi running a PPO agent pre-trained in a Monod + Haldane simulation. Every 30 minutes it reads dissolved oxygen and sets LED brightness and nutrient dosing, then retrains daily on its own jar's data. Grew 42.8% more biomass than a PID controller and 94.7% more than static control (p = 0.002).
